Before your fitting

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A few practical details to help you start with confidence. Garment feasibility, pricing and timing should be confirmed for your specific request.

Can I bring a reference photo or saved design?+

Yes. Save the designs you like in the design library, then include them in your fitting brief. Add your preferred colour, neckline, sleeve shape, embroidery and occasion so the conversation starts with useful details.

Can a design be adapted to my preferences?+

The enquiry is a starting point for discussing customisation. Final choices depend on the garment, fabric, construction, fit and level of detail, so confirm what is feasible during your consultation.

How much will my garment or alteration cost?+

Cost depends on the garment, fabric, embroidery, construction and alteration complexity. Share the garment type and reference details to request a quote before committing to the work.

How early should I enquire before an event?+

Timing varies with the garment and the amount of handwork and fittings required. Include your event date in the enquiry so the studio can confirm a realistic schedule before you proceed.
