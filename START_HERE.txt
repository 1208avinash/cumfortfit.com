CUSTOM FIT PRIME — OPENING THE WEBSITE

1. Keep index.html and the assets folder together.
2. Double-click OPEN_CUSTOM_FIT_PRIME.bat, or open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
3. Allow internet access for the four-scene stitching film and web fonts.

The film is a short browser montage that streams four clips in sequence. The site is a local prototype; appointment requests are not yet sent to a studio inbox.
